# CPT Myth Buster

## Overview
CPT Myth Buster is an evidence-based research project that organizes common questions and misconceptions about Curricular Practical Training (CPT). It demonstrates responsible AI-assisted research: identifying claims, locating authoritative sources, distinguishing general information from individualized advice, and communicating findings clearly.

## Purpose
Create a structured, source-led resource that helps students understand common CPT questions while emphasizing that immigration decisions require official university and legal guidance.

## Research workflow
1. Define a precise question or claim.
2. Prioritize authoritative sources, including USCIS and designated school official/university guidance.
3. Record source date, scope, and relevant language.
4. Separate verified information from interpretation and open questions.
5. Draft a plain-language summary with explicit limitations.
6. Avoid legal conclusions and direct readers to qualified advisers for personal cases.

## Portfolio evidence
This project showcases source evaluation, research traceability, structured prompting, concise writing, risk awareness, and responsible use of AI tools.

## Folders
- `research/`: source log, question templates, and research notes
- `outputs/`: sanitized final summaries and visual explainers

## Disclaimer
This repository is educational and not legal advice. Immigration rules and school policies can change. Users should consult their university international student office/DSO and, where appropriate, a qualified immigration attorney.
