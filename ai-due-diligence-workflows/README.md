# AI Due-Diligence Workflows

## Overview
This project documents a repeatable, AI-assisted due-diligence workflow for evaluating a business, claim, opportunity, or market question. The emphasis is on traceability, source quality, uncertainty management, and decision-ready communication—not unsupported AI output.

## Core question
How can AI accelerate research while preserving verification, source traceability, and sound business judgment?

## Workflow
1. Define the decision, audience, and scope.
2. Break the question into verifiable claims and evidence needs.
3. Gather primary and credible secondary sources.
4. Evaluate source quality, recency, conflicts, and gaps.
5. Use AI to organize, compare, and summarize evidence with citations retained.
6. Log assumptions, limitations, red flags, and unresolved questions.
7. Produce a concise recommendation, confidence level, and next-step plan.

## Tools
AI-assisted research, source evaluation, structured prompting, spreadsheet documentation, Excel/Google Sheets, presentation tools

## Portfolio evidence
Shows analytical judgment, risk awareness, research synthesis, data storytelling, and responsible AI adoption—skills relevant to finance operations, compliance, strategy, and data roles.

## Folders
- `research/`: evidence logs and source-evaluation notes
- `templates/`: reusable due-diligence templates
- `outputs/`: sanitized summaries and presentation examples
